# Queen’s Blood Classic v1.2.0

Unofficial fan project. Play all 33 World Tour challengers across 9 regions (including the 6 Keepers of the Cruor), 22 Classic-candidate Card Carnival puzzles, the Shinra-8 tournament, Gold Saucer challenges, and the Shadowblood Queen. Includes live hover move preview, classic floating battle numbers, pixel shatter destroy VFX, pre/post-match banter, CRT scanline filter, `QBC1-` deck share codes, Rank 1–12 Blood Hunter progression, 11 languages, and Windows desktop resource management. Card art and local music can be generated or mounted from resources you provide; no original game assets or private audio are distributed.

非官方同人项目。体验横跨 9 大区域的全部 33 名世界巡礼对手（含 6 名赤红血守护者）、22 关 Classic 候选卡牌嘉年华、游轮锦标赛、金碟挑战和暗影血女王。包含实时悬停落子预览、经典战斗跳字、像素碎裂摧毁特效、赛前/赛后蓝窗对话、CRT 扫描线滤镜、`QBC1-` 牌组分享码、Rank 1–12 血之猎人段位、11 种语言及 Windows 桌面资源管理。玩家可使用自行提供的资源生成或挂载卡图与本地音乐；发行包不含原版游戏素材或私人音频。
